Welcome to KeelMac

KeelMac needs Full Disk Access to see caches, logs and other folders macOS protects. It never reads file contents, except to compare files when you look for duplicates.

  1. Click Open Privacy & Security.
  2. Turn on KeelMac under Full Disk Access. If it isn’t listed, click the plus button and choose KeelMac in Applications.
  3. Come back here. KeelMac notices the change on its own.
